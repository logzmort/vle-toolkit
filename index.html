03 / UNDERSTAND

Follow the mathematics

Every curve is a sequence of equilibrium calculations. Inspect one calculation, or the rows that form the graph.

Sampled graph data

What the model assumes

Each species follows yᵢP = xᵢPsatᵢ(T). The liquid solution and vapor are ideal, and liquid-pressure corrections are neglected.

Antoine constants describe pure-component vapor pressure. They do not establish that a mixture is ideal or that its liquids are miscible. Activity coefficients, azeotropes, liquid–liquid separation, critical behavior, and solid phases are not predicted.

How data ranges work

Each species may have several Antoine fits. The first valid fit is used where fits overlap. The graph breaks at fit changes and unsupported gaps; it never silently fills a missing range.

A mixture calculation requires a fit for every species with a nonzero mole fraction. Pure endpoints can therefore extend beyond a binary mixture’s shared range. Extrapolation is optional and clearly marked.
